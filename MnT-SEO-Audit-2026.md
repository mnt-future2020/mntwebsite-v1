# MnT — SEO Audit & Opportunity Plan
### mntfuture.com · Healthcare + E-Commerce software development
*Full-site (pre-launch) audit · Keyword & competitor data: Semrush, June 2026 (US + India)*

---

## Executive summary

MnT's site is a **technically excellent, well-structured pre-launch build** — arguably stronger on-page than most of the agencies currently ranking. Clean URL architecture, a disciplined hub-and-spoke internal linking model, unique keyword-mapped titles/metas on all 20 pages, and `Organization` + `Service` + `FAQPage` + `BreadcrumbList` schema are already in place. The foundation is **strong**.

The three highest-impact priorities now are:

1. **Ship it and get indexed.** The single biggest issue is that the site isn't live/indexed yet. Nothing ranks until it's deployed on HTTPS with the sitemap submitted to Google Search Console + Bing. This is a same-day fix.
2. **Attack the low-difficulty, high-value long-tail first.** Semrush keyword-difficulty (KD) data confirms the strategy: `custom healthcare software development` (KD 9, $21 CPC), `abdm integration` (KD 0), `healthcare saas` (KD 6), `ecommerce app development` (KD 9) are all winnable fast — while head terms (KD 26–28) take 6–12 months.
3. **Build the content/authority engine.** The site has zero blog/guide content. Competitors rank with listicles and cost guides. Without a content layer, the money pages will struggle to gain authority.

**Overall assessment: strong foundation, needs traffic + content.** The build quality is not the bottleneck — deployment, content depth, and link authority are.

## 3. Content gap recommendations

The site is **all money pages, no authority layer**. Competitors (Arkenea, Inventorsoft, Cabot, Dinarys) rank with blogs and listicles. Priority gaps:

1. **ABDM / FHIR pillar guide + cluster** — *Why it matters:* KD ~0, rising demand, global competitors ignore it (India-specific). This is MnT's defensible moat. *Format:* pillar guide + 4 supporting articles (ABHA/HIP/HIU explained, FHIR for founders, ABDM certification steps). *Priority:* **High.** *Effort:* substantial (multi-day).
2. **Cost guides** — "How much does it cost to build a telemedicine app / an e-commerce website". *Why:* highest-converting informational intent; founders search this at the decision stage. *Format:* long-form guide → CTA to money page. *Priority:* **High.** *Effort:* moderate (half-day each).
3. **"How to choose a [healthcare/e-commerce] software development company"** — *Why:* commercial-investigation intent, links to hubs, listicle-adjacent. *Priority:* **High.** *Effort:* moderate.
4. **Comparison pages** — "Shopify vs headless vs custom", "Custom vs off-the-shelf healthcare software". *Why:* decision-stage, table-rich (AI-Overview friendly). *Priority:* Medium. *Effort:* moderate.
5. **Real case studies** — replace the three placeholders. *Why:* E-E-A-T, conversion, link-bait for clients' PR. *Priority:* High. *Effort:* moderate.
6. **Get listed on directory + listicle results** — Clutch, GoodFirms, G2 and third-party "top companies" listicles rank on page 1 for the head terms. *Why:* fastest path to head-term visibility while DA builds. *Priority:* High. *Effort:* quick–moderate.
